# Football arena assets

Both assets are CC0 (public domain). No attribution is required; credited here anyway.

- `footballer.glb` — the Mannequin and its animations from **Universal Animation Library** by Quaternius
  (https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html), CC0 1.0. Trimmed to six clips,
  finger channels and UVs removed, meshopt-compressed.
- `stadium_01_1k.hdr` — **Stadium 01** HDRI by Sergej Majboroda on Poly Haven
  (https://polyhaven.com/a/stadium_01), CC0 1.0.

The stands, crowd, floodlights, LED boards and grass detail are generated in code.
